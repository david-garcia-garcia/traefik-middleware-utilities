Verification: Dragonfly v1.40.0; Redis 8.6.4.

Connection commands: PING — Fully supported.
Scripting: EVAL, EVALSHA, SCRIPT LOAD — Fully supported.

Note: "Fully supported" does not imply byte-for-byte identical behavior.
